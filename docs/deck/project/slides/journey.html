WHAT IT DOES

One complaint, from message to Top 10

“paise kat gaye lekin order nahi bana, please check” · sent on chat

1

Arrives

From chat, email, a phone call or a social post, in any language.

2

Understood

A real complaint about payments. Money lost, high impact.

3

Recognised

Linked to the customer, so a repeat contact counts once.

4

Filed

Under “Money debited but order not placed”, in about a second.

5

Ranked

#1 in the Top 10: 67 customers affected, Critical.

A problem nobody has named? Once 3 different customers report it, it is named and ranked on its own.

Money lost or a safety risk? Flagged urgent from the very first report.

Show this on the second laptop while you demo the prototype. Click once per step as the app does it. 1, it arrives: any channel, any language, here Hinglish on chat. 2, it is understood: the system decides this is a real complaint, that it is about payments, that money was lost and the impact is high. Praise, spam and questions are set aside here. 3, it is recognised: the complaint is tied to the customer, so someone who emails, calls and tweets counts as one person, not three. 4, it is filed under the right recurring problem, typically in about a second. 5, it moves the ranking: this problem is number 1 in the Top 10, 67 customers, Critical, and every score has a written reason. Last click: the system also finds problems nobody has named yet. Once three different customers report the same new thing, it gets a name and a rank by itself. In our test it found all five problems we hid. And a single report of lost money or a safety risk is flagged urgent straight away. Do not name the models here; that comes later.
