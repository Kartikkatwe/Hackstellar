Pause, then drop the numbers one click at a time. One: 65 to 85 percent cheaper than having an LLM read every complaint. Two: 96 percent fewer output tokens: most of our work is choosing, not writing, so we produce almost no output. Three: 2.3 times faster. If asked how this was measured: our side was measured on our test run with the real models; the LLM-everywhere side is projected from calibrated token counts, not run. If asked about tokens overall: the total token saving is smaller than the cost saving, because most of our tokens go to a decision model that is far cheaper per token than an LLM.
