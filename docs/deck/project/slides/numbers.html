Pause, then drop the numbers one click at a time. One: 65 to 85 percent cheaper than the cheapest LLM-everywhere setup, Claude Haiku 5.5: 65 to 76 percent cheaper even when Haiku gets the best prompt caching, 85 percent without caching. Two: 96 percent fewer output tokens. Across the whole run we wrote 11,159 output tokens; reading every complaint with an LLM would write about 295,000. Most of our work is choosing, not writing, so it produces almost no output. Three: 2.3 times faster on average and 3.5 times at the median: a typical complaint is filed in 0.55 seconds, while one LLM call alone takes 1.9 seconds or more. Last line, say it out loud: our numbers were measured live; the comparison side is projected from calibrated token counts, not run. If asked about tokens overall: we use about 55 to 60 percent fewer tokens in total, but most of ours go to a decision model that is far cheaper per token than an LLM, so the cost saving is bigger than the token saving.
