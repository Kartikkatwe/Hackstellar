OUR IDEA

Cheapest step first.

Each complaint stops at the first check that can file it. The LLM only sees what nothing cheaper could decide.

Our idea in one line: a funnel, cheapest step first. A decision model sorts the complaint, a free local model matches it against known problems, a cheap yes/no check handles the unsure ones, and only the few left over reach the LLM. In the live run, 96 percent never reached an LLM.
