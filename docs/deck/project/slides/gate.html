The gate is one call to Jev, a decision model, with six questions: is it a complaint, which of the seven areas, impact, emotion, money lost, legal or safety risk. A decision model fits because every answer is a choice, not writing: it returns probabilities, so an unsure answer is passed on instead of guessed, and it costs three thousandths of a cent per complaint. The questions come from the business's settings file, so a new business needs no training.
