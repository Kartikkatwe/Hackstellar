The ranking is a written formula, not a black box. Customers, not messages, times average impact from 1 to 4, times one plus the share with money lost, times a small anger factor capped at 1.25 so a loud customer never outranks a quiet one who lost money, times 1.25 if the problem is rising. Rising means at least 5 customers in the last 7 days and at least double the week before. Example: duplicate payment debits, 52 customers, scores 547.3.
