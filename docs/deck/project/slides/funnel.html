Walk left to right. 1 Intake turns four channels into one shape and works out who the customer is. 2 The gate, Jev, drops non-complaints and picks the area. 3 Similarity match, local and free, files most complaints on its own. 4 Jev's yes/no check files the unsure ones. 5 Only the last few reach the LLM. 6 Anything new waits in the buffer until 3 different customers report it; then the LLM names a new issue. Every confirmed verdict becomes an example, so the system gets cheaper as it runs. After each batch the Curator merges duplicates, and the ranker builds the Top 10.
