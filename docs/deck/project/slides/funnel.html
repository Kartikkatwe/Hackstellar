Walk left to right. 1 Intake turns four channels into one shape and works out who the customer is. 2 The gate, Jev, drops non-complaints and picks the area: 10 percent dropped. 3 Similarity match, local and free, files half of all complaints. 4 Jev's yes/no check files another 35 percent. 5 Only 1.7 percent reach the LLM. 6 Anything new waits in the buffer until 3 different customers report it; then the LLM names a new issue. Every confirmed verdict becomes an example, so the system gets cheaper as it runs. After each batch the Curator merges duplicates, and the ranker builds the Top 10.
