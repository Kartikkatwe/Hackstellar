THE PROBLEM

Complaints arrive everywhere, worded every way

Four channels

Email, phone calls, chat and social posts, each in its own format.

A hundred wordings

“paise kat gaye lekin order nahi bana” is the same problem as “money debited, no order”.

One customer, many messages

Someone who emails, calls and tweets is one customer, not three.

A business needs to know three things: which problems keep coming back, how many customers each one hurts, and which customer groups are hit hardest. That is hard because complaints come from four channels in different formats, the same problem is written a hundred ways in English, Hindi and Hinglish, and one angry customer who emails, calls and tweets is one customer, not three.
