The obvious design is to send every complaint to an LLM. It works, but every complaint waits on the model, at least 1.9 seconds measured on Claude Haiku. If the provider is down or rate-limiting during a complaint spike, nothing gets filed. And it costs about $594 per 100,000 complaints on Sonnet. The 1.9 s is our measured Haiku call time with a short prompt; an everything-prompt is longer, so it is a floor.
