This is our own estimate, so the assumption is shown openly: we don’t know the share of orders that lead to a complaint, so we show 1, 2 and 3%. At 7.8 million orders a day, that is 2.3 to 7 million complaints a month across quick commerce. Our model cost is 100 to 300 dollars a month; even an LLM on every complaint is only a few hundred more. So the buyer’s problem is not token cost, it is contact volume. If fixing the top issues removes just 1% of complaints, that is 23,000 to 70,000 fewer contacts a month. Its value is the operator’s own cost per contact, which we will measure in the pilot rather than guess.
