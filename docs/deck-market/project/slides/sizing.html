This is our own estimate, so the assumption is on the slide: 2% of orders lead to a complaint. That gives 1.5 to 4.7 million complaints a month across quick commerce. Our model cost for that is 65 to 203 dollars a month; even an LLM on every complaint is only a few hundred. So the pitch to a buyer is not token cost. It is fewer contacts: if finding and fixing the top issues removes just 1% of complaints, that is 15,000 to 47,000 fewer assisted contacts a month. At Gartner’s global median that is worth up to 0.6 million dollars a month; Indian contact centres cost less, so treat it as a ceiling.
