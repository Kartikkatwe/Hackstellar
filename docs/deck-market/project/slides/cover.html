HACKSTELLAR PS-19 · MARKET AND SCALE

Complaint Compass

Market opportunity and scalability strategy for a classifier-first complaint engine

13 November 2026

This deck answers two questions: is there a market worth entering, and can the engine scale to it? Short answers: yes, a $2.6 to 2.9 billion core market growing about 10% a year, entered through Indian quick commerce; and yes, by adding copies of stateless workers and giving each business-and-area partition one writer. Market figures come from analyst summaries found by web search; our own figures come from the live run on 1,500 complaints.
