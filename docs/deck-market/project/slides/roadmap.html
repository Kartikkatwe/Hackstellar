ROADMAP

Four phases, each unlocked by evidence

Phase 1 is the priority: it turns the prototype into a production service for one operator, alongside the shadow pilot that clears Gate 1.

We only build capacity after evidence. Phase 0 is today: built and measured. Phase 1 serves one business at up to about a million complaints a month, on Postgres, a queue and two worker types, at about 43 dollars of model cost. We move on only when a shadow pilot passes on held-out labelled data. Phase 2 serves many businesses at 10 million plus a month, once a load test passes and the first paid contract is signed. Phase 3 optimises for 100 million plus, after a second domain pack is live. Engineering spend always trails proven demand.
