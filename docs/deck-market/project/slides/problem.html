Sources: PIB / Department of Consumer Affairs; RBI Annual Report of the Ombudsman Scheme 2024-25; Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020. Gartner puts a median assisted contact at $13.50.

Complaints are growing in both consumer and regulated sectors. The National Consumer Helpline averaged about 1.14 lakh dockets a month in 2024, and e-commerce alone produced 4.46 lakh grievances in one year. The RBI Ombudsman saw 13.34 lakh complaints in FY25, up 13.55%, and those are only the ones that escalated past the bank. The e-commerce rules turn slow triage into a compliance risk: acknowledge in 48 hours, resolve within a month. Underneath, the same fault is written a hundred ways in English, Hindi and Hinglish, across four channels, and one customer often complains on several of them.
