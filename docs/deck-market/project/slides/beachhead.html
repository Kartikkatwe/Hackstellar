Sources: Outlook Business (CareEdge); Storyboard18 (Flipkart and Bain: $7B in 2024, ~40% a year); GAEE Indian Quick Commerce Industry Report.

Quick commerce is the right first market for four reasons. It is growing fast: order value roughly doubled in a year to 64,000 crore rupees, with CareEdge projecting about 2 lakh crore by FY28. It is high volume: between 2.5 and 7.8 million orders a day depending on the source. It is concentrated: three platforms hold most of it, so a few contracts cover the segment. And its complaints are short, repetitive and time-critical, money debited, items missing, products expired, which is exactly what our funnel files fastest.
