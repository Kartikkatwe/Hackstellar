Quick commerce is the right first market for four reasons. It is growing fast: order value roughly doubled in a year to 64,000 crore rupees, with CareEdge projecting about 2 lakh crore by FY28. It is high volume: 7.8 million orders a day in January 2026, according to RedSeer. It is concentrated: three platforms hold 98% of it, so a few contracts cover the segment. And its complaints are short, repetitive and time-critical, money debited, items missing, products expired, which is exactly what our funnel files fastest.
