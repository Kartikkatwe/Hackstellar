The big suites price per agent seat or by custom enterprise quote. Sprinklr retired its self-serve plans and now sells only by quote; Zendesk starts at 19 dollars per agent a month, 55 for its Suite, with quality checks as a per-agent add-on. Medallia and Chattermill price by feedback volume, and Qualtrics doesn’t publish enterprise pricing. Note that these sources are blogs by competing vendors, so treat them as indicative. We don’t replace these suites. We sit underneath as the triage engine and feed the helpdesk they already use, priced per complaint processed.
