The big suites price per agent seat or by custom enterprise contract, and their public material leads with dashboards, sentiment and agent quality. We do not try to replace them. We sit underneath as the triage engine: we file every complaint, de-duplicate customers across channels, discover new issues and rank them by a published formula, then feed the helpdesk they already use. Our proposed pricing is per complaint processed, which undercuts seat pricing and grows with the customer. One honest note: public material doesn’t tell us whether incumbents count unique customers across channels, so we claim that as our strength, not their weakness.
