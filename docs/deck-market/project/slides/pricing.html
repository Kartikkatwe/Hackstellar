Prices are assumptions to test in the shadow pilot, not decided. Volume assumes 2% of 7.8M daily orders. Vendor pricing: Chattermill and eesel blogs.

Our proposed model is usage-based: a platform fee plus a charge per complaint processed. Three reasons. It contrasts with incumbents, who price per agent seat or by custom quote. It matches our cost structure, which is per complaint. And the value we create is per complaint: faster detection and fewer repeat contacts. As an illustration only, at 0.2 to 0.5 cents per complaint, an operator with 4.7 million complaints a month would pay 9,400 to 23,500 dollars, and our model cost would be 1 to 2% of that. The real price comes out of the pilot, measured against what the operator spends handling complaints today. Margin depends on hosting, which we measure in Phase 1.
