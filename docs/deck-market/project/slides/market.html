Three markets frame the opportunity. The core is complaint management software, about 2.6 to 2.9 billion dollars in 2025, growing 8.5 to 10.5% a year. Next to it, AI in call centres is about 2.2 billion and growing 17% a year; voice is one of our four channels. Around both sits customer experience management, 14 to 22 billion, which is the analytics budget we compete for. Analyst numbers differ a lot because each firm scopes the market differently, so these are ranges. We excluded one report that sized an AI complaint segment larger than its own parent market.
