Our test data is template-generated with a fixed seed; the engine never sees the answer key.

The biggest risk is not scale, it is accuracy on real complaints. Our data is template-generated, so it is cleaner than reality, and our cut-offs were tuned on the same data we scored them on. The fix is the same for both: tune on one labelled sample from the customer and report accuracy on a separate one. Jev’s endpoint is still in alpha, so we have a degraded mode and keep all gate logic in one file. Rate limits at production volume are unknown until we load-test. Learned examples could drift an issue, which the cap of 25 and a weekly re-check contain. And incumbents could copy the idea, which is why we integrate with them rather than fight them.
